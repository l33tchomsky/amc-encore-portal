Primary Services

Specialized Appraisal Solutions

Each service type is handled by credentialed appraisers who specialize in that product — ensuring accuracy and defensible values.

🏗️

New Construction

Completed by examining the estimated value of the home using plans and specifications compared to nearby comparable sales.

🏚️

REO / Bank-Owned

Completed for bank-owned properties after the foreclosure process to determine the current value of the asset for the lender.

🔨

Renovation (203K / Homestyle)

Allows borrowers to buy and pay for desired renovations under a single loan — supporting Fannie Mae, FHA, and Freddie Mac programs.

📅

Retrospective Appraisal

Performed at the present time with an effective date in the past, developed from historic data relative to the appraisal's effective date.

🔄

Reverse Mortgage

A complete FHA appraisal is required to obtain a reverse mortgage. Our credentialed team handles this specialized product with precision.

🔍

Appraisal Review

An independent evaluation of an appraisal report ensuring USPAP compliance as part of a quality control and risk management program.

Alternative Valuation Products

Beyond the Traditional Appraisal

Modern valuation solutions for lenders who need flexibility without sacrificing accuracy or compliance.

📊

Value + Inspection Report

Based on a comprehensive real estate dataset and informed by an onsite condition assessment. A certified local data collector conducts the review — exterior only, interior + exterior, or full condition report with rehab budget.

🏠

Property Condition Report (PCR)

Obtained by a local, trained data collector. Includes material characteristics of the subject property with interior and exterior photos, and optional sketch/floorplans.

📝

Broker Price Opinion (BPO)

Completed by a real estate broker or agent combining data collection with MLS or public records. Not USPAP-required — ideal for portfolio management and internal risk analysis.

🔎

Field Review

A new appraiser completes an inspection of the subject and comparable sales, determining whether the original opinion of value is supported — and providing their own if not.
