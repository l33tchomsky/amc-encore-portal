How It Works

Additional Performance

Encore has accumulated millions of data points from thousands of appraisers across every county in the United States — encompassing crucial performance metrics and predictive analytics for future performance.

Order Received

Lender or broker submits an appraisal order with property details, loan type, and timeline requirements.

Predictive Matching

Our algorithm evaluates the appraiser's past performance with the same loan and product types, as well as the precise property location.

Assignment & Tracking

The order is assigned to the highest-performing appraiser — removing subjectivity and ensuring quality before work begins.

Delivery & Reporting

Final report delivered with a full performance transparency report — so you always know why Encore outperforms.
