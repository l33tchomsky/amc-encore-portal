About Encore

The Values We Live By

Built by appraisers, for the industry. AMC Encore was founded on the belief that performance, quality, and transparency should never be sacrificed for profit.

Why Encore? Why Now?

The Encore Difference

"The valuation industry is undergoing the largest, most disruptive transition in history. AMCs have been consolidated by private equity companies who are focused solely on profits, not performance and quality."

Qualified appraiser numbers are rapidly shrinking. Add in the new UAD 3.6, new URAR forms, and appraisal modernization — all of which further complicate the landscape. Who you choose to partner with today will make all the difference in your business tomorrow.
